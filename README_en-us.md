# From Information to Competence

A practical project exploring the use of generative AI in educational content creation, from concept development to final publication.

## About the Ebook

**From Information to Competence: Why Learning Is Harder Than It Seems** is an essay designed for students and young professionals. It explores common barriers to learning and discusses how to turn information into understanding, retention, and applicable skills, using accessible language and an intellectually honest approach.
